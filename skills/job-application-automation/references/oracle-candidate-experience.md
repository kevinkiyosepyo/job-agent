# Oracle Candidate Experience: validated entry and profile-import path

Use this as a supplement to the umbrella workflow for Oracle Candidate Experience/Honeywell pages.

## Verified path

1. Confirm the official listing's employer, title, requisition, eligibility, pay, and deadline; deduplicate before changing the application.
2. Click `APPLY NOW`.
3. On the email gateway, enter the candidate email and acknowledge the required privacy notice. Oracle can create/update a candidate profile without a pre-existing account.
4. On the profile page, upload the approved resume through **Import your profile from resume**.
5. Wait for the page to report the profile import completed. Resume parsing may take several seconds and re-render the page. Re-read parsed first name, last name, and email before relying on them.
6. Address fields are dependent controls: start with Country and verify a bound selection before filling State, County, City, and ZIP. Typed text alone is not a selected value.
7. Use only profile-backed or explicitly confirmed answers for legal and employment-history questions. A CAPTCHA remains a manual gate.

## Verification

Never call an application submitted unless the post-submit confirmation/candidate-home entry is visible and the tracker is read back from Sheets.
